# Air Fryer Hamburgers

## Ingredients

* 1 pound lean ground beef 80/20
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
* 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
* 1/4 barbecue sauce (optional)
* 2 hamburger buns
* toppings as desired

## Instructions

1. Combine beef, salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlic powder in a medium bowl
    1. **Ensure you use meat with a little bit of fat (80/20) so the burgers stay moist.**
2. Form into 4 patties, about 1/2" thick. Make a small indent in the middle of the patty with your thumb. Brush patties with BBQ sauce if using.
3. Preheat the air fryer to 370F. Add patties in a single layer to the basket.
4. Cook 6 minutes. Flip burgers over and cook an additional 3-5 minutes or until beef reaches 160F.
    1. **Don't overcook: use a meat thermometer to ensure the thickest part of a patty reaches 160F.**
    2. **Appliances can vary, so check your burgers early to ensure they don't overcook.**
    3. **Frozen burger patties can be cooked in the air fryer. They'll need to add about 5-7 minutes, depending on thickness. Use a meat thermometer to ensure doneness.**
5. Top with cheese if desired. Cook 1 more minute before serving. Serve on buns.
    1. **If you prefer them more well done, cook longer. If you prefer them with a bit of pink, cook for a little less time.**
